Giraffes

The giraffe is the world's tallest land mammal, standing up to 19 feet high. Its iconic six-foot neck contains just seven vertebrae, matching the human skeletal structure. A unique coat of brown, patchy spots provides excellent savanna camouflage. Their heads feature small, horn-like ossicones and a strong, prehensile 18-inch blue tongue used for grasping food. Long, powerful legs support their massive frame,with the front legs stretching slightly higher than the back. Here is an example of how a giraffe looks like!
